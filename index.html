Getting started

  • Host: tap Start a round, connect Apple Music, choose a playlist, and tap Start music. The host needs an Apple Music subscription.
  • Guest: tap Join a round and scan the QR code on the host's screen, or type the 6-character code. No account or subscription needed.
  • Music plays from the host's phone, through whatever speaker it's connected to.

Common questions

My partner's requests aren't showing up. Both phones need a signal. Requests made without one are saved and sent as soon as the phone is back online.

The app closed in the middle of a round. Open CartDJ again and tap Resume round (host) or Rejoin (guest) on the home screen. The round, code and queue are kept.

How do I delete my account? Open Profile and tap Delete account. This removes your account and its data.
